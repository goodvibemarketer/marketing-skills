What 400,000 Claude Code sessions show
Anthropic's data on who actually gets results from AI.
1 Every job can code
Every major occupation succeeded at nearly the same rate as software engineers.
2 Domain wins
Users who knew their domain hit verified success 2× as often as novices.
3 You plan, it builds
People made ~70% of planning decisions. Claude made ~80% of execution.
4 Experts keep going
When it went wrong, novices gave up 19% of the time. Everyone else, 5–7%.
5 Expertise multiplies
The more expertise a user brought, the more work Claude did per instruction.
6 Competent is enough
Most of the gain came from novice to competent, not to world-class.
