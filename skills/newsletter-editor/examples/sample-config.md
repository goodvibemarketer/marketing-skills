# Newsletter Config

**Newsletter name**: The Retrofit
**URL**: theretrofit.co.uk
**Publication day**: Wednesday

## Niche
Practical decarbonisation strategy for UK facilities managers, property directors, and sustainability leads at mid-sized commercial landlords.

## Audience
UK property operators responsible for the net-zero transition of existing commercial buildings. Mid-career, technically literate, budget-constrained, sceptical of greenwash. They are executing on retrofit programmes, not debating whether climate change is real.

## Voice rules
- UK English throughout
- No em dashes (use commas or full stops)
- No hype language (banned: game-changing, revolutionary, unlock)
- First-person mandatory in the article section
- Short paragraphs, one to three lines
- Sceptical but practical tone, never preachy
- Use concrete numbers over abstractions wherever possible
- Avoid the word "journey" when describing transitions

## Section titles
- **News section**: The Weekly Briefing
- **Article section**: The Long Read

## Article length target
Medium (1,200 to 1,800 words)

## Notes
Author background: 15 years in commercial property, chartered surveyor, now runs retrofit consultancy. Reader should assume the author has done this work, not just written about it.
