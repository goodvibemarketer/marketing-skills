# first-names.txt

A merged, lower-cased list of common first names in English-speaking countries, used only to check whether part of an email address looks like a first name.

Source: the `@stdlib/datasets-female-first-names-en` and `@stdlib/datasets-male-first-names-en` datasets (stdlib-js), derived from Grady Ward's Moby Word II. The data is released under the Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication & License 1.0, and its contents under Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (public domain).
